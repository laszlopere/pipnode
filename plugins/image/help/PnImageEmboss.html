Emboss

Group: Image. Type: PnImageEmboss. Plugin: pipnode-image.

Transform node. It runs a directional 3×3 emboss convolution on the image's luma with a mid-grey (+128) bias, turning the picture into a grey relief where edges look raised or carved depending on their orientation, and emits the result as a new PnImageMessage. Any alpha channel is preserved.

The pixels are processed in plain C against the raw image buffer; no external program is run. A message that is not an image passes through unchanged.
