Sun Path

Group: Sinks. Type: PnSunPath.

Draws the message from an Astronomical node as a small, rotatable 3D sky dome. A translucent ground disc carries the N/S/E/W compass marks, a single reference house sits at the centre, and the Sun's full-day track arcs over the dome — solid where the Sun is above the horizon, dashed where it dips below. A Sun glyph rides the arc at the live position the message reports. Wire it straight off an Astronomical node's output; it needs nothing more than one message to draw the whole day, because it recomputes the arc locally from the resolved latitude/longitude using the same NOAA solar-position math the source node uses.

The card is the same size as the Weather Report and Graph cards, so the three read as a family on the canvas.

Rotating the dome

The scene is genuinely three-dimensional. Click the card to lift it into the centred zoom overlay, then drag to orbit the camera: dragging left/right spins the view a full circle (yaw), dragging up/down tilts it between a near-horizon and a top-down look (pitch). A plain click on the lifted card (without dragging) drops it back onto the canvas. The chosen view is remembered — it is saved with the worksheet and restored on load.

Member readUse
data.success When false the card shows No position and the reason from data.output.
data.latitude / data.longitude The place the Sun's daily arc is computed for.
data.sun_azimuth / data.sun_altitude Where the Sun glyph rides the arc, and the alt/az readout.
data.sun_up Whether the Sun glyph is drawn bright (above the horizon) or dimmed (below).
data.city / data.country The place name shown in the top-left corner.
PropertyDescription
show-house Draw the reference house at the centre of the dome (on by default).
background-color / background-color2 / background-gradient The sky fill: a flat colour, or a linear gradient between the two colours in the chosen direction.
ground-color Fill of the translucent ground disc (its alpha controls how much shows through).
path-color Colour of the Sun's daily track arc.
sun-color Colour of the Sun glyph.
text-color Colour of the compass labels and the place / readout text.

The camera angles are stored as the hidden view-yaw and view-pitch properties; you normally set them by dragging rather than in this dialog.
