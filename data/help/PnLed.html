LED

Group: Sinks. Type: PnLed.

A status indicator shaped like a real 5 mm through-hole LED clipped into a black panel-mount bracket, drawn inset on the right side of the node header. The mode property selects one of four behaviours. Flash (the default) lights the LED on every incoming message and self-extinguishes on a timer, so it reads as an activity blink. Steady (level), Blink Slow and Blink Fast are all level-driven: they latch the LED to the boolean carried on data.value and either hold it on (Steady) or oscillate while on at ~1 Hz (Slow) or ~4 Hz (Fast). Useful as a heartbeat / liveness widget at the end of a pipeline — wire the Flash mode after a filter to see at a glance that messages are still flowing, or wire a level-driven mode after a Threshold / Comparator to show a latched on/off condition (Steady for a quiet readout, Blink Slow for a heartbeat, Blink Fast to draw the eye to a fault).

Modes

Flash — every message turns the LED on in its configured colour and (re)arms the off timer; the LED stays lit until the hold period elapses with no further messages. A steady stream of messages keeps it continuously lit. This is the historical behaviour and the default.

Steady (level) — the LED follows the number on data.value: it lights when the value is above the 0.5 midpoint and goes dark at or below it (the same boolean-on-value convention Threshold and Comparator emit). A message that carries no numeric value leaves the lamp where it is. No timer is involved, so the lamp holds its level until the next value arrives.

Blink Slow — same level latch as Steady, but while the latched value is on the LED oscillates at ~1 Hz (500 ms on, 500 ms off). When the value falls at or below 0.5 the LED goes dark and the blink stops. A messages without a numeric value leaves the latch alone.

Blink Fast — identical to Blink Slow but oscillates at ~4 Hz (125 ms on, 125 ms off) — the rate hardware indicator LEDs use for "activity" or "fault" reads, so this mode pairs naturally with a fault condition wired through a Threshold.

The hold-ms property is only meaningful in Flash mode; the three level-driven modes ignore it, and the settings dialog greys it out while any of them is selected.

The bracket is a thick black ring with a thin lighter highlight on its upper-left rim so it reads as a chunky moulded bezel. The coloured dome sits recessed inside the bracket (a hairline shadow at the seam sells the recess), filled by a radial gradient from a bright centre to a darker rim. While lit, a soft halo of the LED's colour spills out around the bracket for the "glowing" read; while off, the dome fills with two greys so it reads as a translucent window with no light behind it. A small upper-left catch-light keeps the 3D look even in the off state.

Timing

In Flash mode the hold period (hold-ms) is the minimum time the LED stays lit after the most-recent message. Every new message resets the timer, so a steady stream of messages keeps the LED continuously lit. The minimum value is 100 ms — below that a one-shot flash would not be reliably visible on a 60 Hz display. The hold period has no effect in Steady or the Blink modes.

Properties
PropertyDescription
mode Flash blinks the LED on each message for the hold period; Steady (level), Blink Slow and Blink Fast latch the LED to the boolean on data.value (holding the level, oscillating at ~1 Hz, or oscillating at ~4 Hz respectively). Default: Flash.
color Colour the LED glows in while lit. The unlit face is always a neutral grey — only the lit state uses this colour. Default: a bright green, the canonical "OK / activity" indicator.
hold-ms Minimum hold time in milliseconds after the most-recent message (Flash mode only; ignored in Steady and the Blink modes). Floor of 100 ms. Default: 250 ms.
