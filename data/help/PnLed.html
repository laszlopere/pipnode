LED

Group: Sinks. Type: PnLed.

A simple activity indicator shaped like a real 5 mm through-hole LED clipped into a black panel-mount bracket, drawn inset on the right side of the node header. Every incoming message turns the LED on in its configured colour; it stays lit until the configured hold period elapses with no further messages arriving. Useful as a heartbeat / liveness widget at the end of a pipeline — wire it after a filter to see at a glance that messages are still flowing, or after a network probe to confirm a remote host is still responding.

The bracket is a thick black ring with a thin lighter highlight on its upper-left rim so it reads as a chunky moulded bezel. The coloured dome sits recessed inside the bracket (a hairline shadow at the seam sells the recess), filled by a radial gradient from a bright centre to a darker rim. While lit, a soft halo of the LED's colour spills out around the bracket for the "glowing" read; while off, the dome fills with two greys so it reads as a translucent window with no light behind it. A small upper-left catch-light keeps the 3D look even in the off state.

Timing

The hold period (hold-ms) is the minimum time the LED stays lit after the most-recent message. Every new message resets the timer, so a steady stream of messages keeps the LED continuously lit. The minimum value is 100 ms — below that a one-shot flash would not be reliably visible on a 60 Hz display.

Properties
PropertyDescription
color Colour the LED glows in while lit. The unlit face is always a neutral grey — only the lit state uses this colour. Default: a bright green, the canonical "OK / activity" indicator.
hold-ms Minimum hold time in milliseconds after the most-recent message. Floor of 100 ms. Default: 250 ms.
