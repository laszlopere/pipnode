Weather Report

Group: Sinks. Type: PnWeatherReport.

Sink that renders the message emitted by a Weather node as a compact, mostly-monochrome weather card. Like the Graph node the header is drawn in the standard Node-RED style and a white card of the same dimensions sits below it with a small gap; instead of a plot the card is painted with cairo and Pango — FontAwesome glyphs and plain text — to lay out the place, the current conditions, the temperature, and a row of detail tiles. The look is deliberately black on white: a clean, print-like report rather than a dashboard.

The card has three bands. A location row at the top shows a map-marker, the resolved “City, Country” (ellipsized when long), and the observation time on the right. The headline band in the middle pairs a large condition glyph (sun, moon, cloud, umbrella, snowflake, or bolt, chosen from the WMO weather code and the day/night flag) with the temperature, a short description, and a “feels like” sub-line that also surfaces precipitation when there is any. The detail tiles along the bottom show humidity, wind (a small compass arrow drawn to the reported bearing, plus the speed and cardinal direction), pressure, and cloud cover.

The node also mirrors the current conditions onto its own header glyph, so the small at-rest node on the canvas reflects the weather at a glance. Clicking the card lifts it into the centred zoom overlay at the same 280:173 aspect ratio for a larger read.

Input

Wire the output of a Weather node straight into this node's input. It reads the named members the Weather node promotes (city, country, temperature, humidity, wind_speed, weather_code, description, success, output) and dips into the raw Open-Meteo passthrough at data/raw/current for the extra fields it surfaces (apparent_temperature, is_day, cloud_cover, pressure_msl, precipitation, wind_direction_10m, and the observation time). Missing fields are shown as an em-dash rather than a zero. Before the first message arrives the card shows a “Waiting for weather” placeholder; a message whose success is false shows the failure reason from output instead.

PropertyDescription
show-details When enabled (the default), draws the bottom row of detail tiles — humidity, wind, pressure, and cloud cover — under the headline conditions. Turn it off for a minimal card that shows only the place and the headline temperature / conditions.
temperature-unit Unit the temperature and feels-like values are shown in: Celsius (°C) (default), Fahrenheit (°F), or Kelvin (K). The Weather node always reports Celsius; the card converts on display.
wind-unit Unit the wind speed is shown in: km/h (default), m/s, mph, or knots. Converted from the km/h the Weather node reports.
pressure-unit Unit the air pressure tile is shown in: hPa (default, equivalent to mbar), kPa, inHg, or mmHg. Converted from the hPa the Weather node reports.
