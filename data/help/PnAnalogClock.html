Analogue Clock

Group: Sinks. Type: PnAnalogClock.

Paints a round analogue wall-clock face: an off-white dial inside a slim metallic bezel, twelve chunky black hour markers, sixty thin minute pips around the rim, and three drop-shadowed pivoted hands (hour, minute and an optional seconds). A configurable caption sits below the centre pivot in the place a manufacturer's logo would normally occupy on a real wall clock.

Logic is the same as the Digital Clock: each incoming message must carry a numeric value holding a count of seconds (the Unix epoch the Clock node emits is the canonical feed); the node takes it modulo a day and sweeps the hands as a 24-hour clock (the hour hand makes two full turns per day). When value is zero or negative, or before any message has arrived, the hands park at 12 o'clock. Being a sink, the node never forwards a message; a non-numeric value is ignored and leaves the current reading untouched. The face repaints only when the visible hands actually move.

The timezone combo shifts the wall-clock readout by a fixed UTC offset, chosen from the same abbreviation-and-name list the Digital Clock and Deadline nodes use (e.g. CET — Central European Time). Daylight-saving variants are listed as their own offsets (CET vs CEST), so no daylight-saving guessing takes place. The default is Not Set: with no fixed zone selected the node reads the incoming message's timezone member as a bare abbreviation (which the Clock node emits as the local zone, e.g. CEST) and applies the matching offset. An unknown, missing or ambiguous abbreviation falls back to GMT (UTC+0).

SettingDescription
text Short caption drawn below the centre pivot in place of the manufacturer logo a real wall clock carries. Empty to suppress.
show-seconds Draw the thin seconds hand. Switch off for a quieter two-hand face that only updates once a minute.
timezone Timezone the face is shown in, chosen from a list of abbreviations and names. Not Set (the default) defers to the incoming message's timezone member; an unknown or missing abbreviation falls back to GMT.
face-color Fill colour of the dial face — the white face of a wall clock by default.
marker-color Colour of the twelve hour markers and the sixty minute pips around the rim — black by default.
hour-hand-color Fill colour of the short, broad hour hand.
minute-hand-color Fill colour of the long, narrower minute hand.
second-hand-color Stroke colour of the thin seconds hand — the classic bright red of a wall clock by default.
text-color Colour of the configurable caption below the pivot.
