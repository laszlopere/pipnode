Sound

Group: Sinks. Type: PnSound.

Plays a short clip whenever a message arrives. Playback is handed to paplay; bare sound-theme ids are resolved to /usr/share/sounds/freedesktop/stereo/<id>.oga. Two clips never overlap: messages that arrive while a previous clip is still playing are dropped, as are messages that arrive within the configured dead period after a clip ends. Incoming message contents are ignored — the trigger fact alone is what plays the sound.

PropertyDescription
sound The clip to play, set either by picking an entry from the combobox of system sound ids or by browsing to an audio file. Empty means "not configured" and paints the node red.
dead-period Seconds of mandatory silence after each playback. While this window is active incoming messages are dropped, which is useful when an upstream node fires faster than the clip should retrigger.
This node depends on paplay (PulseAudio) being installed; system sound ids additionally require the freedesktop sound theme under /usr/share/sounds/freedesktop.
