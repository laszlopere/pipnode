Jump Out

Group: Routing. Type: PnJumpOut.

The exit end of a named connection. Whatever arrives at any Jump In whose tag matches this one is emitted from this flag's output port, anywhere in the document. See Jump In for the full contract — the two nodes are one feature.

The flag has no input: it is driven only by its partners, never by a wire. Disabling it drops the messages destined for it without disturbing the other flags on the same tag.

A flag with no tag, or one whose tag no Jump In feeds, is painted red with a warning mark — an output nobody drives is almost always a typo in the tag.
