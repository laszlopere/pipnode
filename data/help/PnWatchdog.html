Watchdog

Group: Filters. Type: PnWatchdog.

Auto-triggered silence detector. Incoming messages are consumed — they are never forwarded — and only set an internal “saw a message since the last tick” flag. On every tick the flag is read and cleared: if no message arrived during the previous interval the watchdog emits a fresh message with data.success = false and data.output = output-text. Wire it after a flaky upstream stream and into a Debug or TTS sink to be told when the stream goes quiet.

PropertyDescription
period Tick interval in seconds; the maximum tolerated silence.
output-text Text placed in data.output on alarm.
