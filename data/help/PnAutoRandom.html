AutoRandom

Group: Sources. Type: PnAutoRandom.

Auto-triggered sibling of AutoInjector that fills data.value with a freshly-sampled random number every period seconds instead of a fixed configured one. The sample range and the shape of the draw are configurable, so a freshly-dropped AutoRandom is ready to feed a Graph with a synthetic noisy signal, drive a Filter stress test with a known distribution, or stand in for a yet-to-be-built probe whose expected dynamic range is already known. Each instance carries its own seeded RNG so two AutoRandoms placed on the same worksheet produce independent streams.

Every draw is clamped to [min, max] regardless of the distribution chosen, so downstream rules can rely on the emitted value sitting inside the configured range. The emitted message carries data.value (the sampled number), data.output (the configured text) and data.success (the configured boolean) — the same canonical members AutoInjector uses, so the two are interchangeable for filters and sinks downstream.

PropertyDescription
min Lower bound of the sampled range.
max Upper bound of the sampled range. If min is accidentally larger than max the bounds are swapped silently rather than refusing to draw.
distribution Shape of the draw on [min, max]:
  • uniform — mass spread evenly across the range. Default.
  • normal — Gaussian centred on the midpoint with stddev = (max - min) / 6, so ~99.7% of draws fall inside the range before the boundary clamp catches the tails.
  • triangular — symmetric, peaks at the midpoint and falls linearly to zero at both bounds. The pdf is naturally bounded so the clamp is effectively a no-op.
  • exponential — heavily biased toward min, with lambda = 3 / (max - min) so the mean lands at min + (max - min) / 3 and ~95% of mass falls below max; the tail is clamped at max.
output String emitted as data.output on every tick.
success Boolean emitted as data.success on every tick.
period Seconds between draws.
