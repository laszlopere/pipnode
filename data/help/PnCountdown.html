Countdown

Group: Sinks. Type: PnCountdown.

Paints a digital countdown clock — a black panel of seven-segment LED digits laid out as DAYS  HOURS:MINUTES:SECONDS, the look of a wall-mounted event countdown display. Each incoming message must carry a numeric value holding the seconds remaining until the target instant — exactly what the Deadline node emits. The node breaks that total into days, hours, minutes and seconds and lights the digits accordingly.

When value is zero or negative (the deadline has passed), or before any message has arrived, the display shows all zeroes. Being a sink, the node never forwards a message; a non-numeric value is ignored and leaves the current reading untouched.

A typical chain is Clock → Deadline → Countdown: the Clock ticks the current epoch once a second, the Deadline turns it into the seconds remaining, and the Countdown paints the result. The display repaints only when the visible digits actually change, so a once-a-second feed drives exactly one redraw per second.

SettingDescription
day-digits How many seven-segment cells the days field uses (1–6). Three (the default) counts up to 999 days; a days count larger than the field can show saturates at all nines rather than wrapping.
show-labels Draw the DAYS / HOURS / MINUTES / SECONDS captions above their digit groups.
background-color Fill colour of the panel face behind the digits — the near-black face of an LED clock by default.
segment-color Colour of a lit segment bar — the classic LED red by default.
unlit-segment-color Colour of an unlit segment bar — the dark off-state ghost of a digit, a dim red over the black face by default.
label-color Colour of the DAYS / HOURS / MINUTES / SECONDS captions — a soft off-white by default.
