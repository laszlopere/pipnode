Clock

Group: Sources. Type: PnRtc.

Real-time-clock node. Emits one message per period seconds carrying the current local-time decomposition in its data bag: value (the Unix epoch, also exposed as a precise int64 in epoch), year, month, dom, hour, minute, second, and timezone (the DST-aware local zone abbreviation, e.g. CET/CEST). The value member lets the tick plug straight into nodes that read the standard numeric payload. Useful as a heartbeat or a tick generator that drives downstream work on a schedule.

PropertyDescription
period Seconds between emissions. Defaults to 1.
